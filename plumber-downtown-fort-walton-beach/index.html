The downtown district

Downtown Fort Walton Beach is a district and not a residential subdivision. The City of Fort Walton Beach has a Downtown Master Plan and a Community Redevelopment Agency that works on the area. City Hall is at 107 Miracle Strip Parkway SW, and Fort Walton Beach Landing Park sits at 139 Brooks Street SE with about 700 feet of shoreline on Santa Rosa Sound.

The Landing has hosted the Billy Bowlegs Pirate Festival, other festivals and concerts. According to the city, a renovation there includes a new seawall, a boardwalk, a dock and a kayak launch, with a stage and new restrooms planned for a later phase. Events like these bring crowds that put real demand on restrooms and kitchens nearby.

Plumbing for restaurants, shops and mixed-use buildings

Commercial plumbing differs from residential work because many people use the same fixtures and the building may have several tenants. Restaurants and bars add grease and food waste to the drain. Retail and offices add restroom load. Older commercial buildings may have been adapted over time, so a drain or supply line may not match the original drawings.

We make no claim about the age or condition of particular downtown buildings. If you manage a property, a camera look at the drain and a walkthrough of the fixtures show what you actually have. See sewer camera inspection and commercial plumbing.

Commercial plumbing topics

These are general topics for commercial buildings. They are not claims about particular downtown properties.

Kitchen and floor drains

Grease and food solids build up in drain lines and traps. Cleaning and jetting keep them moving. See drain cleaning and hydro jetting.

Grease management

Many places require a grease trap or interceptor for food service. Ask your utility and your building department which rules apply to you.

Restroom fixtures

High use wears flush valves, faucets and supply stops faster than home use. See toilet repair and faucet repair.

Water heaters

Kitchens and restrooms often need steady hot water. See water heater repair.

Match the problem to the likely cause
