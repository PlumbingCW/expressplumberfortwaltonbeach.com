Where Elliott Point is, and where the name gets fuzzy

According to local real estate guides, Elliott Point is a residential area near downtown Fort Walton Beach. One guide names SE Hollywood Boulevard, SE Waynel Circle and SE Scottwood Drive as streets in it, and another places it around Brooks Street and Bay Drive on the eastern side of town. We checked addresses on Hollywood Boulevard SE, Scottwood Drive SE and Waynel Circle SE against U.S. Census place boundaries, and they fall inside the City of Fort Walton Beach.

The name also belongs to Elliott Point Elementary School, which the city traffic study places inside the neighboring Ferry Park neighborhood. In everyday use the two names overlap. We use the street names above and do not claim an official boundary.

Mixed ages and remodeled homes

The guides describe homes that range from mid-century houses to larger updated ones, with water access for some. We found no county data on age or materials, so we make no claim about what is in your walls.

A remodeled house often has mixed materials. New copper or PEX may connect to older drain lines, and a past owner may have moved a heater or added a bath. If you are buying or planning work, ask for a record of what was changed. A camera look at the drain and a check of the supply lines tell you more than the year on the deed. Plumbing topics for houses near the water

These are general plumbing points. They are not claims about particular failures in Elliott Point.

Outdoor spigots and irrigation

Properties with yards or dock access often have several outdoor connections. A running hose bib or a cracked irrigation line can raise a bill quietly. See leak detection.

Exposed fittings near salt air

Metal fittings outdoors and in garages can corrode sooner than indoor ones. Look at them each season. See water line repair.

Water heaters in garages and closets

Check the base of the tank for rust trails or damp. See water heater repair.

Kitchen and bath drains

Slow drains are the most common call for any house. See drain cleaning.
