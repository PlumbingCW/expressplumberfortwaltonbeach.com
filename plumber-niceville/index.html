Clogs and sewer backups come first in Niceville

The calls we hear most from Niceville homes are about drains and sewer lines. Large lots with mature trees put roots near buried pipes, and many houses have long runs from the house to the street. A slow drain that returns after cleaning usually has a root or a belly in the pipe behind it.

The first step is a cable through the cleanout to restore flow. The second is a camera to see why it happened. Our sewer line cleaning and sewer inspection pages explain both steps.

Sewer backup in Niceville: do these first

A backup that rises through a floor drain or tub is urgent. The 24/7 line is open, and these steps limit the damage.

Stop all water use

No flushing, showers or laundry until a plumber arrives.

Locate your cleanout

It is often a capped pipe near the house or by the street.

Keep children and pets away

Backed-up water carries contamination.

Call and describe the pattern

Tell us which fixtures are affected and when it began.

Do not open the cleanout cap if sewage is already rising. The plumber will do that with the right protection.

Utilities and what we can say about the water
