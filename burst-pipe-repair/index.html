First hour of a burst pipe

A burst pipe can release hundreds of gallons in an hour, so speed matters more than anything else. The 24/7 line is open, and the steps below cover what to do before the plumber arrives.

1

Close the main valve

Turn it clockwise to off. A ball valve closes with a quarter turn of the lever.

2

Open the lowest faucet

This drains the pipes and relieves pressure.

3

Turn off the water heater

Shut off power or gas so the tank does not heat without water.

4

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets, switch off the breaker and stay clear.

5

Call the 24/7 line

Describe where the water is and what the shutoff did.

If you cannot find or turn the main valve, call anyway. The plumber can guide you over the phone or shut it at the meter.

What causes a pipe to burst on the Gulf Coast

Hard freezes are rare on the Panhandle, but they do happen, and an unprotected exposed pipe can split in a cold snap. Most bursts here come from something else. Old copper develops pinholes, plastic fittings fail with age and sun exposure, and a pressure spike from a failing regulator can burst a weak joint. Corrosion at hose bibs and exposed lines near the coast is another factor.

If water pressure at your house is high, you may notice banging pipes or leaks at several fixtures over time. A pressure gauge on a hose bib shows your static pressure, and normal household pressure is commonly in the range of 40 to 60 psi. Anything far above that is worth a plumber's look.

What kind of pipe failed

The pipe material changes the repair. Here is how the common types compare.

PipeCommon failureTypical repair
CopperPinholes, split from pressure or freezeCut out the section and sweat in a new piece or use a coupling
PEXFitting leak, sun damage, rodent chewReplace the section with a crimp or expansion fitting
CPVCBrittle crack at a joint or fittingReplace the section, often with transition fittings
Galvanized steelInternal corrosion and thin wallsReplace the section or plan a repipe
Cast iron drainCrack or corrosion holeReplace the section with PVC or use a no-hub coupling

If more than one pipe has failed in the same house, ask about repiping instead of a series of patches.

A burst pipe in the ceiling or wall

Water inside a wall or ceiling does more damage than the break itself. Shut the main immediately and place buckets under any drips. Open a faucet downstairs to drain the line.

Wet drywall and insulation can mold within a day or two in humid conditions, so call a water mitigation company if the area is large. We repair the plumbing, and for drying and restoration you will need a separate contractor.

Signs a pipe is about to fail

Pipes often give warning before they burst. These signs are worth a call.

Blue-green stains on copper

Corrosion at joints or pinholes.

Discolored water

Rust or corrosion inside older metal lines.

Banging or hammering pipes

High pressure or loose lines stressing the system.

Damp patches or bubbling paint

A slow leak inside a wall.

Sudden drop in pressure

Water is escaping before it reaches your taps.

A small drip is the earliest warning. It can turn into a burst when the pipe finally gives way.

Immediate safety for a burst pipe

A repaired supply line has to meet the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC). Water that reaches outlets or the panel is a National Electrical Code (NEC) hazard, so keep out of it.

What to do now: close the main valve, open the lowest faucet to drain the line, switch off power to wet areas only if it is safe and photograph the damage for your insurer.
