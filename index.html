What counts as an emergency plumber call

An emergency plumber is the right call when waiting until morning would cost you more than the call does. Water pouring from a burst pipe, a toilet overflowing, sewage backing up in a tub, a water heater leaking onto the floor and a gas smell near an appliance all qualify. Our 24/7 line is open for every one of them, and a dispatcher will help you decide what can wait.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. For everything else, the first steps are the same. Close the main valve if water is running, stop using drains if they are backing up and call. Our burst pipe repair page walks through the first hour in detail.

Plumbing around the Santa Rosa Sound

Fort Walton Beach sits between the Santa Rosa Sound to the south and Choctawhatchee Bay to the north. Coastal air is hard on exposed fittings, water heaters in garages and outdoor hose bibs, and sandy soil with a high water table keeps buried pipes in moist ground. Tree roots from oaks and pines chase that moisture into older drain lines.

Many homes in the area sit on concrete slabs. When a line under the floor leaks, the signs are a warm patch, a spinning meter and a rising bill, and our slab leak repair page explains how those are found and fixed. Hurricane season runs from June 1 through November 30, so knowing where your main shutoff is makes a real difference.

Landmarks, roads and where we go

Our plumbers work across the city, from the downtown streets around Heritage Park and the Indian Temple Mound Museum to neighborhoods along US 98 and State Road 189. Buildings downtown range widely in age, so we check the pipe material before we quote. Giving the dispatcher a cross street or a nearby landmark such as Liza Jackson Park or the Fort Walton Beach Medical Center helps the plumber find you faster.

Okaloosa Island is reached across Brooks Bridge, and vacation rentals there can leak for days before a guest reports it. Owners who fly in through the Destin-Fort Walton Beach Airport often call from the road, and a local contact with a key makes the visit quicker. State Road 85 carries traffic north toward Crestview and Interstate 10, and calls from that side of the city are handled just the same.
