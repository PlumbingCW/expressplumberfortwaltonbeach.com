What to tell the dispatcher

A few details speed up the call. Give your address and the nearest cross street. Describe what is happening, what you can see and what you have already done, such as closing the main valve. Say who can let the plumber in if you will not be home.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first. Do not use electrical switches or phones inside until you are outside. Then call us for the plumbing repair once the area is safe.
