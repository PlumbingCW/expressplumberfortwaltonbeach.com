Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you visit this website or call our line.

Information we handle

This website does not use forms and does not ask you to submit personal details. When you call our line, we may collect your name, phone number, service address and details about the plumbing problem so that we can dispatch a plumber and give you a price.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

How we use information

We use the details you give us to book and carry out service, to follow up on a job and to improve how we answer calls. We share your name, phone number and address with the plumber assigned to your job so that the work can be done.

We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

Website data

Our website is hosted by a third-party provider that may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver and secure the site. Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices and how to reach us

You can ask us what information we hold about your job, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Write to contact@expressplumberfortwaltonbeach.com or call (855) 575-2890.

We may update this policy from time to time. The version on this page is the current one.
