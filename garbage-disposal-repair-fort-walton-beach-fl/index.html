Garbage Disposal Unit Repair in Fort Walton Beach, FL

A garbage disposal that hums, jams or leaks often has a simple cause. This guide covers the safe checks, the repairs worth attempting and the signs that a disposal needs replacing in a Fort Walton Beach, FL kitchen.

How a disposal works and why it fails

A disposal does not have blades like a blender. A spinning plate with small lugs flings food against a grinding ring, and the pieces wash through the ring into the drain. When something hard or fibrous wedges between the plate and the ring, the motor hums but cannot turn.

Other failures are electrical or mechanical. A tripped reset button, a failed switch or a worn bearing all stop the unit. A leak means a seal or a housing has failed, and that is a replacement, not a repair.

Clearing a jam safely

Always cut power before you touch a disposal. These steps work for most jams.

1

Switch off and unplug the unit

Turn off the wall switch and unplug under the sink if you can.

2

Look for the obstruction

Use a flashlight and tongs to remove anything visible. Never reach in with your hand.

3

Use the hex key

Insert a hex wrench in the socket at the bottom and turn it back and forth until the plate moves freely.

4

Press the reset button

A small red button on the bottom pops out when the motor overheats.

5

Run cold water and test

Turn on the water, then the switch. If it hums again, stop and call a plumber.

If the unit has no hex socket, a wooden spoon handle from the top can turn the plate. Keep your hands out of the opening.

Symptoms and next steps

This table shows what each symptom suggests and whether a repair is practical.

Many disposals last about ten years. If yours is older and has started to fail, replacement is often the better value.

What belongs in a disposal

Small, soft scraps are fine. Fibrous peels, bones, grease, coffee grounds in large amounts and starchy foods such as rice and pasta cause problems. Shellfish shells and fish skin are especially hard on a unit and on the line behind it. If your family cooks a lot of seafood, scrape shells into the trash.

Run cold water during use and for several seconds after. Cold water keeps fats solid so they are chopped and carried away. Hot water melts fat, which then coats the pipe downstream.

When the drain behind the disposal is the real problem

A disposal that runs fine while the sink drains slowly is a drain problem. The pipe behind it may be coated with grease. A cable or hydro jetting clears it. Our drain cleaning page explains the options. If you are seeing water in the dishwasher side of the sink, the drain line is the likely cause as well.

Choosing between repair and replacement

A disposal that hums and will not turn can often be freed with a hex key at the bottom of the unit. One that leaks from the housing seam usually cannot be saved, because the body has failed. A rule that works well is to repair a jam and replace a leak.

Age matters too. If the unit is several years old and has already needed a fix, a new one with a similar horsepower costs little compared with repeated visits. Homeowners in Destin and Mary Esther who rent out a property tend to replace sooner, since guests rarely follow disposal rules.

Whichever way you go, check the drain trap under the sink at the same time. Grease in the trap can mimic a failing disposal.

The trap and drain behind a disposal follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC), and the power connection follows the National Electrical Code (NEC).

Fort Walton Beach local note: city and county sewer

Many homes in the area are connected to city or county sewer, and some rely on septic tanks. Septic systems are particularly sensitive to grease and food waste, so a disposal adds to the load. Ask your utility or the county whether your home is on sewer or septic, and follow the guidance for your system.

Your water and sewer bill names the utility that serves you. The City of Fort Walton Beach and Okaloosa County Water and Sewer each publish information for their customers.
