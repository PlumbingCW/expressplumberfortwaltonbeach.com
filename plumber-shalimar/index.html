Bayfront lots and water in the ground

Shalimar sits on the north side of the area along the bay, and low lots near the water can hold moisture in the soil. That matters for slab foundations and for buried drain lines. A pipe that sits in wet sandy soil can shift, and roots from yard trees add to the strain. Heavy storms during hurricane season, from June 1 through November 30, can test older joints.

For a house with repeated backups, start with a drain cleaning visit and ask for a camera look. If the line is failing, sewer line repair covers what comes next.
