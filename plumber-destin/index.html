Destin neighborhood by neighborhood

Destin is a small city with several quite different areas, and the plumbing demands differ with them. Here is how we think about the main parts of town.

Destin Harbor and the waterfront

Restaurants, charter businesses and condos concentrate along the harbor. Kitchen drains and grease are the main issues, and the 24/7 dispatch matters for businesses that cannot close.

Crystal Beach and the Gulf side

Beach houses and townhomes see salt-air corrosion on exterior fittings, hose bibs and water heaters. Leaks in an unoccupied home can run for days.

Near Henderson Beach State Park

Homes and condos around the park run from older cottages to newer builds. Pipe age varies from block to block, so we check what is in the walls before quoting.

Miramar Beach edge

Just across the county line, Miramar Beach shares the same resort pattern. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for those calls as well.

Vacation homes and the problem of empty rooms

A house that sits empty between guests can leak for days before anyone notices. A water heater that fails in an unused unit, a toilet flapper that sticks or a supply line that bursts under a sink is found only when the next guest arrives. A smart leak sensor under water heaters and sinks is cheap insurance, and so is a simple walk-through at turnover.

When you call about a suspected hidden leak, the usual first step is a water meter check. Our leak detection page explains the two-hour meter test. For units where an old tank has failed, water heater replacement is the usual fix.

Condos, associations and who calls the plumber

Condo plumbing has layers. The association usually owns the risers and common lines. The owner usually owns the lines inside the unit from the shutoff onward. A leak that crosses unit boundaries involves both. The association rules and your insurance policy decide who pays, so it helps to read them before an emergency.

If the problem is in a restaurant or shop, our commercial plumbing page covers after-hours work, grease traps and backflow testing. For the shoreline towns that sit between Destin and Fort Walton Beach, see our Shalimar and Niceville pages.

Notes on Destin water and heaters

Destin Water Users, Inc. is one of the water systems in the area, and the utility that serves you depends on your address. Your bill names it. Each utility publishes an annual water quality report, and that report is the right place to look for details about your water. We do not quote a hardness figure for Destin, so test your tap if scale on fixtures is a concern.

Tankless heaters are popular in newer condos because they save space. Flushing them on the manufacturer schedule keeps them working, as covered on our tankless water heater page.
