Where we work

The core of our service area is Fort Walton Beach itself, including ZIP codes 32547, 32548 and 32549, along with Okaloosa Island and Wright. From there we reach east to Destin, west through Mary Esther to Navarre, and north across the bay to Shalimar and Niceville.

Hurlburt Field and Eglin Air Force Base sit inside the area, and maintenance for on-base housing usually goes through the base housing office. We focus on homes and businesses off base. Miramar Beach is just east of Destin, and calls from there go through the same 24/7 line.
