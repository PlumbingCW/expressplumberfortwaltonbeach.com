Hurricane season and Navarre plumbing

Hurricane season runs from June 1 through November 30, and Navarre, with its Gulf-side beach and low-lying lots, is a place where storm preparation matters. A short checklist helps before the season. Know where the main water shutoff is, test the sump or ejector pump if you have one, and make sure floor drains are clear.

After a storm, boil water notices and contamination can follow, and tree roots and debris can damage lines. If your plumbing is damaged, call early. A busy season means more calls, so being specific about what is failing speeds things up.
